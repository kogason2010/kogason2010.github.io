# Campus asset credits

## Trees (`trees/`)
- Tree generator in `index.html` is adapted from **EZ-Tree** by Daniel Greenheck
  (https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2024 Daniel Greenheck.
- Leaf textures (`*_leaves.png`, `pine_needles.png`) come from the EZ-Tree package (MIT), resized;
  `aspen_leaves.png` is a hue-shifted copy of its aspen texture.
- Bark textures (`*_bark_*.jpg`) come from the EZ-Tree package, resized. Original sources:
  birch https://www.texturecan.com/details/221/, pine https://www.texturecan.com/details/588/,
  oak https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02 (CC0).

## Grass (`grass/`)
- The grass shading follows "How to Make the Fluffiest Grass with Three.js" (Codrops, 2025) and
  **FluffyGrass** by Ebenezer (https://github.com/thebenezer/FluffyGrass), MIT License,
  Copyright (c) 2023 Ebenezer. `tuft.jpg` is its grass alpha texture, resized.
