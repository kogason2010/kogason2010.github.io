Strand by strand

How a single quadrupole mass spectrometer confirms what a synthetic oligonucleotide is: by its charges, its mass, and where it elutes.

Oligonucleotides are short synthetic strands of DNA or RNA, increasingly used as medicines. The LCMS-2050's DUIS ion source ionizes them by electrospray (ESI) and APCI at the same time.

02 · Many charges

Every linkage
is a charge.

Oligonucleotides carry a phosphate (or phosphorothioate) linkage between neighbouring nucleotides, so they are usually detected as negative ions. One molecule picks up several charges at once and appears at a whole ladder of m/z values.

03 · Ion pairing · SSI note

Shake off
the HFIP.

Ion-pair LC puts HFIP and triethylamine in the mobile phase (here 95 mM HFIP and 4.3 mM TEA). Some HFIP reaches the mass spectrometer still attached, so each charge state splits into [M]z−, [M+HFIP]z− and [M+2HFIP]z−. That clutter makes deconvolution harder.

Of the seven source settings, Qarray voltage had the most substantial effect on signal and adducts. At the default −20 V and below, adducts were high and total signal low. Signal rose up to about −40 V for the 15-mer and about −60 V for the 30- and 60-mer, then fell past −70 V. −40 V was chosen.

04 · Method development · 01-00766

Six strands
that look alike.

The sample mixes a 20-mer full-length product (FLP) with five impurities: two 19-mers each missing one end nucleotide (one at the 3′ end, one at the 5′ end), an n-3 17-mer, an n+1 21-mer, and "PO", the FLP with one phosphorothioate at the 5′ end turned into a phosphate diester. Their UV spectra are almost identical (similarity 0.99 or higher).

Initial screen · tap a cell
Evaluation value = P × (Rs1 + Rs2 + … + RsP−1)

P is the number of peaks found and Rs the resolution between neighbours. LabSolutions MD ranks every run by this value; only the published top 10 carry a number here. Dashed cells showed baseline fluctuation.

05 · Optimization · 01-00766

Chasing
the last 0.44.

Starting from the best screen (100 mM HFIP, 10 mM TEA), three more settings were varied: acetonitrile share of the organic solvent (40, 50, 60%), oven temperature (55, 60, 65 °C) and initial gradient concentration (6, 7, 8%). Resolution generally improved as each went up, though the design spaces showed the best acetonitrile share depended on the pair.

Overlaid design spaces then found the point that maximizes the hardest pair, n-1(5′)/n-1(3′), while keeping n-1(3′)/PO above Rs 0.7 and the last peak before 16 min: ACN 54% / MeOH 46%, 65 °C, initial 8%.

06 · siRNA without ion pairs · 01-01180

A duplex
comes apart.

Ion-pair reagents such as hexylamine or triethylamine raise concerns that instrument contamination may affect other analyses. This note drops them: a C18 column (Shim-pack Scepter Claris C18-300) with 10 mM ammonium bicarbonate in water and a methanol gradient.

The sample is a double-stranded siRNA based on the patisiran sequence, two 21-nt strands.

What came off the column

At both 25 °C and 60 °C the UV trace showed two peaks, not one, so the duplex had dissociated into single strands. Insight Biologics identified them as the sense and antisense strands, each within 1 Da of its theoretical mass.

07 · HILIC · 01-01238

A water layer
instead.

Hydrophilic interaction chromatography needs no ion-pair reagent. On a Shodex HILICpak VN-50 2D column, with 100 mmol/L ammonium acetate mixed 30:70 into water (A) or acetonitrile (B) and a gradient from 92% B downward, a mipomersen-based 20-mer and its n-1(5′) and n-3(5′) variants eluted at 8.432, 8.956 and 9.300 min: shortest strand first.

Quantities from mixed peaks

When peaks overlap, Insight Biologics splits the UV area by each component's share of the MS signal:

component area = UV peak area × MS area of component ÷ sum of MS areas in that peak

The trade-offs

Sensitivity: MS signal was about one-fifth to one-tenth of that in ion-pair reversed phase, so low concentrations remain a challenge.

Buffer: ammonium bicarbonate (pH 8) also worked, but retention times shortened over consecutive runs as dissolved CO₂ from the air changed the mobile phase, and MS signal was about half that with ammonium acetate. Ammonia at pH 10 gave no gain. Ammonium acetate was preferred.

Upside: with no ion-pair reagent, the system need not be dedicated to this one analysis.

What is drawn, and what is data

Tables, masses, m/z values, conditions and equations are copied from the notes. Chromatograms at the bottom of the screen are redrawn: peak positions and heights are read from the published figures and are approximate. Qarray bar values are read from Fig. 1 of the SSI note and are approximate. The 3D strands, adducts, duplex and columns are illustrations, not structures.

"Daltons from this rung" in the charge ladder is simple arithmetic, |z| × m/z ± |z| × 1.00728; the deconvoluted masses are the software's published results.
